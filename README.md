# 💫 About Me:
Currently contributing to software development initiatives at Sanction Trace, where I focus on building scalable and intelligent backend systems. I hold a degree in Software Engineering from Fırat University and have 4+ years of hands-on experience across different domains including cybersecurity, AI-driven systems, and enterprise applications.
 
Throughout my career at Popleads, Imperum, MND Teknoloji ve İzolasyon A.Ş., and Logical Defense, I have developed strong expertise in local LLM implementations, OCR technologies, and modern software architecture. I have worked extensively with Python (FastAPI), microservices, and data-driven systems, always aiming to build efficient, maintainable, and production-ready solutions.
 
Beyond engineering, I bring a product-oriented mindset supported by certifications in product management, cloud & DevOps, and project management. I enjoy bridging the gap between technical execution and business needs, ensuring that what we build delivers real impact.
 
I am passionate about continuous learning, emerging AI technologies, and building systems that solve complex real-world problems.
